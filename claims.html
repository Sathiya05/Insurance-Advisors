Claims FAQ

Questions People Ask During a Claim

Quick, honest answers about timing, documents, and what to expect.

How long does a claim usually take?

Timelines vary by insurer and claim type. Simple claims can settle in days, while complex or disputed ones may take weeks. Your insurer's acknowledgment letter usually states the expected review window.

What if my claim is rejected?

A rejection letter must include the reason. We help you read it, check whether documents or an appeal can change the outcome, and guide you through the insurer's appeals process where one exists.

Do I need to notify you before contacting the insurer?

Not necessarily — you can report directly to your insurer. Many clients prefer to involve us first so we can flag required documents and common pitfalls before anything is submitted.

What does "documents requested" mean in the tracker?

The insurer needs more information before it can proceed — often additional reports, bills, or IDs. Upload or send exactly what was listed to avoid further delays.

Is there a fee for claims assistance?

Guidance through this demo site is free. Any real-world service fees, if applicable, would always be explained clearly before you agree to anything.
